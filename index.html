Before you begin

Consent for future sleep research at The Institute of Sleep Science Kolkata

Please read the information below in full. It explains what this broad consent covers, how your data will be used in future research, and your rights as a participant — including the right to withdraw at any time. You can only continue once you've confirmed you've read it.

What this consent covers

This is a broad consent, as described in the ICMR National Ethical Guidelines for Biomedical and Health Research Involving Human Participants (2017). It is not consent to a single named study. Instead, it covers the future research use of your clinical data and questionnaire responses generated through your ongoing care and treatment at The Institute of Sleep Science Kolkata, as well as any past medical records you choose to voluntarily share with us — for example, in studies on sleep architecture, obstructive sleep apnoea, PAP therapy adherence, or drug-induced sleep endoscopy (DISE). No separate research procedures, trials, or sample collection are involved. It does not extend to research outside this scope, or to sharing your identifiable data with parties outside The Institute of Sleep Science Kolkata, without separately asking you.

Why broad consent, and what stays in place regardless

Asking you to re-consent before every individual future study is often impractical and, ICMR notes, can itself become a burden on participants. Broad consent lets The Institute of Sleep Science Kolkata use already-collected data for related future research without contacting you again for each one — with these protections staying in place regardless:

  • Every future research use of your data must still be reviewed and approved by The Institute of Sleep Science Kolkata's Institutional Ethics Committee before it proceeds, on the same terms as any new study.
  • The Institute of Sleep Science Kolkata will maintain your contact details so you can be reached if a future use falls outside what's described here, or if the Ethics Committee determines fresh consent is warranted.
  • You may withdraw this consent at any time, for any reason, without affecting any care or services you're otherwise entitled to. Withdrawal stops future use of your data/samples from that point on; it doesn't undo research already completed using them.

What this involves

This does not involve any separate research procedures, trials, or sample collection. It covers only the data already generated through your ongoing clinical care and treatment at The Institute of Sleep Science Kolkata — sleep studies, clinical measurements, and questionnaire responses collected as part of your normal management — along with any past medical records or history from elsewhere that you choose to voluntarily disclose to us. Nothing extra is asked of you beyond your regular treatment; disclosing past records is optional and up to you.

Data storage

Identifiable data is retained only by The Institute of Sleep Science Kolkata, in its local and cloud-based storage systems, protected by identity-based authentication and access controls and by encryption both in transit and at rest. Before any data is shared with third-party research collaborators, it is de-identified.

Risks and benefits

Because this consent covers only data from your existing treatment — not any separate research procedure — there are no additional medical risks or discomforts associated with participation. Your data helps further research in ENT, sleep disorder, and pulmonary medicine.

Confidentiality

Your data is de-identified before it is shared with any third-party research collaborator. Identifiable data is never shared outside The Institute of Sleep Science Kolkata and is accessible only within its access-controlled storage systems.

No commercial use

Your data is used for research purposes only. It is not sold, licensed, or put to any commercial use, by The Institute of Sleep Science Kolkata or by any third party it is shared with.

Contact
